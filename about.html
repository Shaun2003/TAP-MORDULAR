About TAP MORDULAR

We make containers work harder.

We turn shipping containers into considered, practical spaces shaped around the people and businesses that use them.

Our approach

Start with the purpose. Shape the space around it.

A good modular building starts with the work it needs to do. We listen to your use case, location and priorities, then discuss a container layout and finish that makes sense for the brief.

From a compact retail outlet to a multi-room facility, we help clarify the practical choices before the project moves forward.

  • Layouts and openings based on your operation
  • Options to buy, or enquire about rental availability
  • Fit-out and services discussed against your needs
  • Delivery location and site access considered early
